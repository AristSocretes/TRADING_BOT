Everything a prop desk does. On autopilot.

◈

Reinforcement learning

PPO policies trained on 300k+ timesteps with per-bar costs, risk penalties and alignment bonuses. Every model is validated out-of-sample with bootstrap significance statistics.

◊

Execution-aware

Almgren–Chriss square-root market impact in training and live simulation. Vol-targeted position sizing. A prop-style max-drawdown floor that locks trading when breached.

✦

Regime detection

A Gaussian hidden Markov model classifies each bar as CALM, TREND, VOLATILE or CHOP using conditional volatility — and scales exposure down when the market turns violent.

Built on the research that runs real desks.

GARCH(1,1) + EWMA volatility

Conditional variance features with per-frequency half-lives — the RiskMetrics recursion, vectorized forward without look-ahead.

Bailey & López de Prado PSR

Probabilistic Sharpe ratio and Lo (2002) standard errors gate which model earns a production slot. No lucky backtests.

Stationary bootstrap

Politis–Romano block resampling produces honest confidence intervals for every out-of-sample Sharpe ratio.

Cross-asset context

Each instrument sees normalized relatives of its market peers — indices, currencies and metals — as part of its observation.
